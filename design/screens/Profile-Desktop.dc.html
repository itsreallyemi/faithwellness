About me

I grew up in a pastor’s home in Lagos and was ordained after seminary in California. For nearly two decades I’ve sat with couples on the edge of giving up and individuals carrying grief they never had room to name.

I believe no story is too broken for grace — and that healing usually happens slowly, in honest conversation, with someone who won’t rush you.

How I bring faith into care

We start where you are. I pair trauma-informed practice with Scripture and prayer — only as much as you want — so faith becomes a source of strength, not pressure. If you need clinical support beyond pastoral care, I’ll help you find it.
